# Independent blind authorship attribution

The acceptance target is that independent reviewers cannot reliably identify the author's originals. Preference, polish, readability improvement, technical export success and a high science score cannot substitute for that target. Do not stop at those secondary outcomes.

## Isolate and freeze

Use immutable raw scientific prompts: complete values/precision, proper method names, prior context, conditions, interpretations and uncertainty. Remove target screenshots, geometry and exact headline wording unless it is a real method name. Verify equations/operators against the full target before locking; text extraction can omit ratios. Corrections create new input conditions and invalidate affected earlier comparisons.

Give a fresh maker only frozen skill, prompts and non-target references. It must not read targets, keys, prior outputs, reviews or the developer conversation. Layout and copy use separate fresh stage packets when authorized, followed by assembly; retain their contracts. Shared filesystem barriers are procedural, not OS security isolation.

Development feedback may revise the skill. A target promoted to training is permanently excluded from fresh validation. Keep at least one new target completely outside revision. Neighboring material containing the identical experiment is not independent training evidence. Preserve every preselected case and failure.

Freeze final persisted files and hashes before review. Lock first review text before revealing source identities. Later supplements use separate files. Never edit a first judgment after source reveal.

## Anonymous pairs

Randomize sides independently for each case. Use equal-size complete renders and identical page markers. Apply the same PDF rasterizer/settings to both arms when available; document PPTX-to-PDF export, font substitution and layout effects separately. Do not mask content, blur a condition, crop away difficult evidence or degrade only one arm to hide provenance.

Fresh reviewers see only their packet: raw scientific facts, relevant non-target style references and anonymous images. No keys, source-indicating paths, implementation or other reviews. They inspect both full-resolution images per pair.

## Required judgment and rationale

For each case record:

1. `humanGuess`: L, R or uncertain; confidence0.50–1.00, uncertain=0.50. Explicitly distinguish no reliable basis from a forced weak guess.
2. Observable authorship cues and counterevidence: exact object/region, typography, composition, narrative cadence, evidence footprint or emphasis. Vague “looks AI” is insufficient. Do not assume cleaner means generated or damage means original.
3. Up to three largest real differences, ranked: severity, exact side/object, scientific impact separately from attribution impact, and a transferable fix. A harmless difference can be highly diagnostic. Do not invent defects to fill a quota.
4. Concrete scientific/rendering gate issues: omitted values, misleading scope, unreadable/clipped evidence or damaged relationships. Do not demand every maker constraint or known acronym as visible prose.

Summarize up to five developer priorities linked to the responsible module: routing/family selection, layout, wording, native construction or rendering. The rationale is observable justification, not privileged knowledge of who made a page. Do not ask which slide they prefer; that is not the user's acceptance criterion.

## Interpret without lowering the standard

Decode source attribution only after locking. Report guesses, confidence, uncertainty and systematic cues per case, separating development regression from fresh transfer. Reliable identification means failure even when all science/technical gates pass. Mixed guesses from one small model-only panel do not prove stable indistinguishability; high-confidence consistently reversed guesses also indicate distinguishable groups, not success.

Iterate the narrow failing module, freeze a new skill, then use a fresh maker and reviewers. Do not manually retouch only the candidate and attribute the result to the skill. Keep source keys and raw rationales in the curator bundle. Document same-model-family, prompt-reconstruction, case dependence and renderer limits. Continue toward the actual authorship test rather than delivering a preference-based pass.
