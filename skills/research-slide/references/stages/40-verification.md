# Stage 4 — verify the actual criterion

Inspect the final saved/reimported artifact under the host finalization contract. Check exact evidence, scope, native editability, glyphs, cell/row fit, connectors and footer. Record real warnings.

Use a fresh science reader to compare the actual saved render with the raw authorized scientific record after repairs. For each public obligation, inspect its planned recovery surfaces together: title/header/table/math/diagram/asset/prose may jointly express a claim. Confirm the recovered meaning retains comparison scope, uncertainty and conditions. A formal definition can supply meaning without an English paraphrase; a local `Sink(S)` label may define the canonical sink-window mapping. Bare unexplained new symbols and hidden-letter pseudo-definitions fail. Familiar PPL/BF16 and standard mathematical conventions need no manufactured glossary.

Inspect cited evidence at presentation size, including exact string precision, NA marks, units, source region and frozen asset identity. Metadata is not visible evidence. Check actual quotations on planned slots/objects rather than trusting IDs or related nouns. Private/context material stays out of visible claims. Detect unsupported or unplanned content semantically; deterministic checks cannot prove its absence.

Check semantic perspective as well as literal coverage. Does an internal research observation speak directly from the work, or narrate what “the researcher reports” because a curator used that wording? Removing the wrapper must retain a reported/tentative explanation's status. Check average-versus-per-task and ranked-set exceptions separately from author fidelity; a compact source sentence that overclaims is evidence of cadence, not permission to overclaim. Do not reward scientific correction as an authorship cue or remove it to imitate the reference.

Run `python scripts/validate_contracts.py facts.json --layout layout.json --copy copy.json` as a structural gate. It cannot detect a scientifically false paraphrase, a mixed semantic claim, a misleading relation between two correct nouns, or illegibility. Record those checks from visible evidence, not a regex or the script's pass status.

Run the narrow [saved-page style check](../saved-page-style-check.md) with a fresh critic and the actual saved-file PNG, selected reference/measurements and minimal argument role/audience. Exclude old reviews, other families, approved copy and full fact dumps. Return only supported mismatches to layout/copy; a separate fresh science reader checks the repaired render against raw science. This internal QA never replaces final blind attribution. Compare with the selected non-target family: title language/absence, bullet glyph/indent, note hierarchy, evidence footprint/orientation, palette, emphasis and page-marker font. Cleaner/larger/more explanatory does not establish author fidelity. Preserve readable author density; repair lost scientific content without manufacturing source damage.

Read title, body and scientific object together. Flag a repeated topic introduction, a familiar-abbreviation glossary added for internal collaborators, or a schematic relation restated across example, row labels, legend and takeaway. Keep the minimum scientifically necessary mapping and the chosen parent/example rhythm; selective inline emphasis should follow the selected language evidence, not turn every lead into a heading. Route a mistaken audience/definition assignment upstream rather than hiding it in assembly.

Route repairs to facts, layout, copy or assembly. Technical success is not style acceptance. Actual Google/PowerPoint execution is distinct from local export/readback. For blind tests freeze everything before review; use [attribution](../blind-evaluation.md). Require observable source cues and ranked differences, separating scientific impact from attribution impact. Preference is not acceptance evidence. Reliable identification means the test failed; preserve it and continue development.
