# Stage 2 — wording inside the approved layout

Input: frozen atomic facts, approved slot plan and the selected example's isolated `copy-style.json`. It includes actual title language separately from body fragments. Read [working language](../copy/working-language.md), [title](../copy/title.md), and only the relevant [experimental claims](../copy/experimental-claims.md), [mechanism text](../copy/mechanism-text.md), or [workflow status](../copy/workflow-status.md). Selected algorithm, paper-outline, procedure and operator families also use [method grammar](../copy/method-procedure.md). Exclude unrelated examples, component APIs, prior generated copy and old reviews.

Treat fact statements as meaning to deliver, not sentences to paste. Preserve the intended collaborator perspective: direct conditions, observations and tentative explanations. Keep the source's confidence and provenance without importing curator agency (“the researcher reports”) into an internal working note. A short “low throughput, attributed to computation overhead” retains an attributed explanation; “overhead causes low throughput” would strengthen it. If attribution to a particular external source matters, retain that attribution.

Output `copy.json` with `schemaVersion: 2`, `caseId`, and `slots` keyed by existing layout slot ids. A slot supplies exact text or native paragraph/run items, levels, local emphasis and `factIds`. If both `text` and paragraphs are supplied, text must equal the paragraph/run text joined with newlines. Retain the selected role grammar: standalone heading, ordinary bullet, inline lead or no heading.

Follow the approved [recovery contract](../recovery-contract.md): `coverage: [{factId, witnesses:[...]}]` repeats actual planned object/slot/evidence locators and quotes actual assigned wording. Joint title/header/table/math/diagram recovery is valid. Read the page objects together before adding prose. Copy writes only the assigned surfaces: do not create an English inventory of meanings already carried by titles, headers, labels or formal equations. A table unit or operator meaning in metadata is not an automatic request for an explanation sentence.

Each newly introduced essential mapping still needs recoverable meaning. For text-based mappings supply the coverage-level `mapping` spans described in the recovery contract; compact equivalent labels such as `Sink(S)` can suffice. Established mathematical definitions can carry meaning directly. A bare symbol or a letter inside an unrelated word cannot witness a definition. The canonical scientific meaning need not be repeated verbatim in full English, but a fresh reader must verify equivalence.

Private/context facts have no public assignment. Evidence-only facts retain their planned evidence surfaces and locators, without new prose. Supported positive comparison scope and actual uncertainty remain recoverable; private production constraints do not become caveats on the page. If the approved recovery plan itself duplicates a meaning, return that conflict to layout rather than filling extra prose slots.

Several fact witnesses may share a sentence or slot. Deliver topic framing once, where the chosen grammar places it: title, inline goal or opening note. Keep a condition/result pair or parent/example hierarchy connected rather than expanding each atomic fact into a standalone explanatory sentence. Use one necessary acceptance/state mapping near a schematic; do not add a legend, count subtitles and a conclusion that all repeat the same relation. If the approved plan requires duplicate framing, an unnecessary glossary or more exposition than its selected grammar, return that specific conflict to layout/facts rather than filling every slot with new prose. Do not silently drop a contracted scientific obligation; change its realization upstream when another object already delivers it.

Keep connected working-note rhythm and current method names. Compress by removing repeated subjects, topic introductions and evidence inventories before shortening scientific scope. Keep “average”, the comparison set, exceptions, controls and uncertainty wherever they change the claim. Familiar audience vocabulary needs no new explanatory tier unless facts deliberately assign it one. A genuine fact/capacity conflict returns to layout; do not delete a required symbol mapping or scientific qualifier to fit. Run `python scripts/validate_contracts.py facts.json --layout layout.json --copy copy.json`, then review visible entailment: witness text must express the fact, rather than merely contain its ID or a related noun. The validator checks structure and literal witnesses, not scientific semantics.
