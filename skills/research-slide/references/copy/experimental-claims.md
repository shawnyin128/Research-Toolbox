# Copy — experimental claims

Bind observations to the actual models, controls, comparisons and scope. Keep interpretations and approximations recognizable. A compact positive scope such as “lowest error among tested compressed variants” bounds the claim without a separate disclaimer. Retain a scientific uncertainty or exception when the record supplies it; a private prohibition against broader claims need not become visible prose.

Let the scientific evidence do its assigned work. Table stubs and grouped headers can identify weight versus activation format, metric families, direction, units and fixed settings. A concise model/settings caption may orient the entire comparison. Do not expand a readable header structure into a “Format denotes … remaining metrics …” paragraph. If the evidence does not recover an essential distinction, add or request a local header/label repair through the approved recovery plan, rather than writing a tutorial around it. Newly introduced essential symbol mappings remain visible somewhere on the page.

Distinguish a pure evidence readout from a fuller experimental account. A table-led readout can consist of a model/settings heading or caption, its complete table, and only the observations required by the report. Follow selected title presence; do not add a synthetic topic heading above a sufficient scientific heading. A fuller account may need separate purpose, setup and result roles. Match the selected hierarchy rather than attaching Experiment/Results to every table.

Write the result at its useful aggregate level, with the material exception local. A higher average “among quantized variants” must not become best across all methods or every task. If AIME24/25 fall while AIME26 rises, preserve that split rather than saying all AIME drops. Mention a task-specific decline that changes an otherwise broad “nearly lossless” reading. These distinctions can share a compact observation; other per-row changes remain in the intact table. Do not enumerate every higher/lower/tied row to prove that the table was checked. Verify scope and any emphasis rule against exact values, including ties.

A result and its reported limitation can share one connected sentence. “Highest average AL among ranked methods; low throughput, attributed to overhead” illustrates cadence only: current facts must support each clause and supply any material exclusion. Keep the method name where needed for unambiguous identity. Avoid restarting the purpose under the title or narrating the researcher from outside. When a family has no result paragraph, do not invent one from provenance alone.
