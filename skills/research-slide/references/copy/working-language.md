# Copy — working language

Keep the researcher's lab terms, method and paper names, and actual prior-page knowledge. Write for the working collaborators: direct observations, questions, tentative explanations and pending work. A curator's reporting language establishes provenance; it need not become the slide's narrator. Preserve a reported explanation's confidence with a concise attribution or hedge. Name an external source when its identity matters to the argument.

Read the selected family's title and body evidence separately. Match its hierarchy: plain observations, bold Experiment/Results roles, numbered concepts, an inline goal, or sparse mathematical labels are different report grammars. A short noun phrase may be a complete stage label. Keep a subordinate example with its parent motivation, and subordinate implementation detail with its numbered step. Selective emphasis belongs on the scientific subject or operative relation evidenced by the family, rather than every lead.

Compress meaning across the whole page. Title, model/settings caption, grouped table headers, equations, object labels and prose jointly carry the scientific account. A fact assigned to a legible header or formula need not appear again in an English sentence. Use the approved object/witness assignments in the [recovery contract](../recovery-contract.md); if they do not deliver an essential meaning, return the specific recovery need rather than adding unassigned prose. Several atomic facts may share one sentence or label. Remove repeated topic framing, metric inventories and notation explanations before changing the approved hierarchy. Retain the conditions, comparison scope, controls, units and uncertainty that change the claim, at a recoverable location near the relevant claim or object.

Familiar internal PPL/BF16/KV and conventional mathematical notation need no automatic glossary. Newly introduced local mappings still need a real definition: a short local label, equation annotation or body span can supply it. Respect established definitions without inventing prior knowledge. Avoid explaining one relationship in the body, node subtitles, legend and caption at once.

For table/plot readouts use [experimental claims](experimental-claims.md). For method outlines, narrative algorithms and numbered implementation reports use [method and procedure](method-procedure.md); for mathematical relations or genuine dependency graphs use [mechanism text](mechanism-text.md). Match directness and hierarchy without copying source experiment values, grammar damage or overbroad claims. Do not invent typos, results, plans or caveats to imitate authorship.
