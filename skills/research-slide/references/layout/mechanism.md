# Layout — definition/mechanism

Let the scientific object carry the page. A continuation may be untitled; a black replacement headline is unnecessary. Preserve the chosen object/prose ratio, scientific typeface, line weight and categorical palette. Geometry/color can establish correspondence without redundant arrows and IDs. Do not add a naming taxonomy for every precision level. Unknown dimensions stay symbolic/ellipsis; within-block counts do not prescribe total blocks. Diagram labels and sustained prose are distinct type roles. Add no tutorial layer merely because a helper supports it.

For one self-contained mechanism plus terse adjacent notes, inspect [compact scientific object](compact-object.md). Preserve independently measured local figure fonts/encodings even when they differ from deck prose. Select by [scientific purpose](family-selector.md), not token presence. Concrete and symbolic annotations may belong inside one object; they do not automatically justify two full-body diagrams or an explanatory footer.


First ask whether the object is the mechanism being taught or a small supporting example in an objective/motivation argument. For the latter, inspect [prose-led overview/inset](prose-led-overview.md): keep the large connected narrative and compact inset instead of allocating a self-contained teaching panel. An explicit scientific relation may already be witnessed by the local shape states and nested prose together; do not force a duplicate legend and conclusion into new slots.
